The chip

A real 8051 is a small black package with 40 metal legs. Every leg has one job. Point at a pin, or tap it, to see what it does.

What to notice

  • 32 of the 40 pins are I/O: four 8-bit ports.
  • Port 3 pins double as serial, interrupt, timer and memory-strobe pins.
  • Only 8 pins run the machine itself: power, reset, the clock and the memory control lines.
  • Software never sees these wires. It sees registers such as P1 and P3, and the next pages show how they connect.
