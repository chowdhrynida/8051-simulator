Why 11.0592 MHz?

The UART makes its bit clock by dividing the crystal. 11.0592 MHz divides into the standard baud rates with nothing left over, so the serial port matches the other device exactly. A 12 MHz crystal cannot. The table shows the baud rate each Timer 1 reload value really gives.
