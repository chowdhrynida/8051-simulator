One instruction

Every instruction is the same three-part loop: fetch the bytes, decode the opcode, execute it. Step through MOV A,#05H and watch the bytes move through the chip.

Ready.

Why step by step?

On the real chip, both bytes of this instruction are fetched inside one machine cycle, which is 12 clock periods long. We slow it down here so you can see each move. The result is the same: A ends up holding the value.
